5.0 // PROCESS

How an implementation project goes

For consulting and training the path is shorter: a free call, then sessions.

Briefly describe the department and the task. The reply is an invitation to a free 30-minute call, where it becomes clear where to start.
The focus is on real processes: what people do day to day and how long it takes. Result: a task map with an estimate of what can be automated. Audit formats and prices → · Automation payback calculator →
Solution architecture and a precise spec for each task: input, output, rules, what must never happen.
Rolled out one task at a time, accepted by whoever used to do it by hand, until the result is stable.
The solution is handed over with documentation. After that, as needed: one-off consultations or the next stage of automation.
