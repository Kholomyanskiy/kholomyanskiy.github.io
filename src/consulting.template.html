AI Implementation Consulting

A review of a specific task with a consultant working on the client's side: whether it is worth automating, where to start, where the risks are. If there isn't enough data to decide, the outcome is a list of what needs to be checked. There is no obligation to order further stages.

The first 30 minutes are free: getting to know the task and choosing the format. The review itself, preparation and the summary are part of the paid meeting.

1.0 // WHO IT'S FOR

When an outside view helps

A manager or owner who needs a second opinion on an idea, a contractor's proposal or a tool already bought.

A specialist who uses AI at work and wants to build a process around their own tasks.

A team considering an audit that wants to find out first whether it needs one.

2.0 // TWO FORMATS

One-off consultation or working session

One-off consultation

Discussing a question, assessing an idea or a contractor's proposal. Usually about an hour.

Outcome: a decision or recommendation and next steps in a written summary.

Working session

Hands-on work on a specific process using the client's materials and tools. Usually one and a half to two hours. Sessions can run as a series.

Outcome: a process worked through, an approach tested or a piece configured. The expected outcome is agreed before the meeting. A finished automation within one session is not promised.

3.0 // HOW IT WORKS

From a free call to a written summary

  1. A free 30-minute call: what the task is and which format fits.
  2. After the call, the date, length, expected outcome and price of the meeting are agreed individually, followed by payment.
  3. Before the meeting the client sends materials and questions, and a plan is prepared from them.
  4. Online meeting in Google Meet or Microsoft Teams.
  5. After the meeting, a written summary: what was decided, what needs to be checked, what the next steps are.
4.0 // PRICE AND TERMS

Payment for agreed time

One-off consultation: from 150 zł/hour, net.

Working session: from 250 zł/hour, net.

Preparation and the summary are included. A one-off consultation covers the question and a small amount of material. Reviewing a large contractor proposal, a repository or a set of documents is agreed separately.

Meeting length, from one hour in 30-minute steps, is agreed in advance. A meeting can be extended by mutual agreement, with each extra 30 minutes paid separately.

Payment in advance for the agreed time. For regular work, a monthly invoice is possible.

A meeting can be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours in advance. A later cancellation or a no-show counts as a held meeting. If the client is late, the meeting still ends at the agreed time. If the consultant reschedules, the client chooses a new date or a refund.

Consultations are held in Polish, Russian and Ukrainian. They are not available in English.

5.0 // SCOPE

What a consultation doesn't replace

A consultation answers a specific question. A full map of the department's processes and an estimate of the effect are part of the process audit. The document a contractor builds the solution from is the specification. Payback can be estimated before the meeting with the calculator. To learn to solve such tasks on your own, see training on your own tasks.
