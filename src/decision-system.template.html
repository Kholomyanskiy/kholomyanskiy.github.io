1.0 // WHAT IT SOLVES

What it solves

Automation often starts with choosing a tool. The process has not been analysed, the payback has not been calculated, and the subscription is already paid for.

The system sets a different order. First the process and the goal, then the expected impact and risks, then the approach to implementation. There is no tool review in it: it is a decision procedure that can be used without a contractor.

3.0 // WHO IT IS FOR

Who it is for

An owner or manager deciding what to automate first. A project or product manager. A business analyst or automation specialist who needs to back a decision with numbers.

Professional is needed if the processes involve personal data, the industry is regulated (finance, healthcare, legal) or the solution has to run on your own server. If none of these apply, Business is enough.

4.0 // HOW TO USE

How to use it

The Business path takes about a week. Days 1–2: Part 1 and choosing one to three processes. Days 3–4: payback for one process, using the examples and the calculator. Day 5: the decision sheet gives an answer, automate or not.

A "yes" leads to Parts 2 and 4 and an implementation plan. A "no" leads to Part 5, why this process is not worth automating, and on to the next candidate.
