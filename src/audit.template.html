1.0 // PROBLEM

Which tasks to hand to AI. Or why AI is not working

The audit answers one of two questions. Which one is yours becomes clear in the free call.

Scenario 1

No AI yet

  • There is a lot of manual work, and management hears about AI everywhere.
  • It is unclear which tasks to hand to AI, which not, and what it would bring.
  • The audit: a task map, an assessment of each task and a calculation of the effect.
Scenario 2

AI is in place, but there is no result

  • A tool was rolled out, but people use it only now and then.
  • Or employees use ChatGPT, Claude or Copilot on their own, and management cannot see what is going on.
  • The audit: why it does not work and what to change, with criteria that show the change.
2.0 // SCOPE OF WORK

What the audit includes

The work is built on documenting processes: data comes from how people actually work, and every number in the report has its source. Stages:

BRIEF

Management call and questionnaire

Goals, scope, pain points, tools already in use. Before work with the team, management explains the purpose of the audit to staff: without this the data gets distorted.

DATA

Collecting data on the work

In a department audit, employees log their tasks for a week: what, how often, how many minutes. In Express, the latest runs of the process are reviewed. Some people take a short interview where the task is shown on screen rather than described.

MAP

Task map

Tasks are consolidated into a process map: who does what, where work is duplicated, where time goes. In scenario 2 the map also lists AI tools: what each is used for and what overlaps.

EVAL

Assessing each task

Each task gets one conclusion with a reason: AI does it, AI assists a person, ordinary automation without AI is enough, keep it human, or put the process in order first. Error cost, the need for human review and failure points are taken into account.

DIAG

Why AI is not working (scenario 2)

Causes are examined on three levels: people (lack the skills, do not see the point, do not trust the output), processes (access, rules, AI not built into the work), tools (the wrong tool, unnecessary subscriptions, overlaps). For each cause: confirmed or not, and what the conclusion rests on.

CALC

Effect calculation

Where the work has been measured: how many hours a month and a year can be freed, how many of them realistically once review and exceptions are counted, and what that is worth in money after tool and implementation costs. Freed-up time is not the same as lower costs, and the report says so.

PLAN

Report and walkthrough

What to do first, what next and what to leave alone. A walkthrough of the report with management is included.

3.0 // OUTPUT

What you get and what is not included

A report for management: the task map, a conclusion for each task with its reason, the effect calculation and the order of steps. In scenario 2 also the causes on three levels and criteria that show after 30, 60 and 90 days whether the situation has changed. In a department audit each employee gets personal recommendations on their own work. In Express, a short report on one process: the verdict and the boundaries of the first step. The report can be taken to any contractor.

All conclusions carry equal weight. "Do not automate", "ordinary automation without AI is enough" or "put the process in order first" are as much an audit result as "hand the task to AI". The audit does not sell implementation and promises neither a percentage of savings nor that anything will be automated.

Not included: staff training, configuring tools and access, ready-made agents, choosing replacement tools, a technical audit of an implemented solution, development, specification, workforce decisions, post-implementation review. Each can be ordered separately.

The next step is chosen from the report: solution design, specification, implementation or training on your own tasks. The criteria in the report also allow a follow-up review later.

5.0 // ENGAGEMENT INVARIANTS

Rules that are never broken

INV-01

The audit's output is a reasoned decision, not a promise. How to use the freed-up time is your call: unburden people, take on more work or redistribute responsibilities.

INV-02

Data is collected with employees' knowledge, and everyone knows in advance what management will see. The audit assesses how the system works, not an individual. Covert observation produces distorted data and a useless calculation.

INV-03

Once the first data is in, the scope is checked against what was agreed. If the standard scope is enough, the audit continues at the same price. If not, you get the reasons and the cost of the extension before any additional work is done.

INV-04

The price is fixed. It is calculated from the completed questionnaire after the free call and set out in an email before payment, together with the scope. No hourly billing.

INV-05

The audit is independent of selling implementation. No percentage of savings is promised up front: the result is known only after the analysis.

7.0 // HOW IT WORKS

How it works

  1. You write or book a free call and briefly describe the task: a process, a department or a situation with AI.
  2. A 30-minute call, free: getting to know the company and the task. After it you receive a questionnaire. No exact price is quoted on the call: it needs the questionnaire answers.
  3. The price is calculated from the questionnaire. The email before payment sets out the format, scope, timeline and price. After the first payment a meeting with management is scheduled.
  4. Data collection. In a department audit, employees log their tasks for a week, which takes a few minutes a day, and some take an interview of about 30 minutes. In Express, a meeting with the process owner and a conversation with one or two people who run the process.
  5. Report and walkthrough with you. Comments on the report are accepted for 7 days and closed in one round of corrections.

Audit materials are processed, among other things, with AI tools; terms and data protection are set out in the contract.
